polling vs push: two ways to hear a tx arrived

ask, or be told

polling what you built

how
ask getrawmempool every N seconds, diff the result
good
simple, near-stateless, works on any RPC
cost
late by up to one interval, and chatty — refetches even when nothing changed

push ZMQ / websockets

how
the node streams each new tx the instant it accepts it
good
near-zero latency, no wasted calls
cost
holds a live connection, must reconnect on drops, misses events during any gap
Poll to learn and to watch low stakes; move to push when latency and volume start costing real money.
