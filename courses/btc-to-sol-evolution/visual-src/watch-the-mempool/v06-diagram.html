the sandwich: one searcher around one victim

wrapped around the trade

price spread = profit
1
searcher BUY token Y
ordered just before — gets the old, lower price
2
victim SWAP X → Y
the large trade — drags the price up
3
searcher SELL token Y
ordered just after — sells into the new, higher price
the takeaway
profit = the spread between step 1 and step 3
