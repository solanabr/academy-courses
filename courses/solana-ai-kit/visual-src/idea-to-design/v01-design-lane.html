The design lane

From three lines of idea to a design an engineer can build from

1
idea.md
A person, what they cannot do today, what your thing does about it. Three lines, written by you.

2
solana-researcher
What exists, what died and why. Answer first, then findings with a link and a label each.
It owes youHigh / Medium / Low / Speculative per finding, and what it could not verify
3
solana-architect
Accounts with owner, seeds, size, rent payer; instructions with signers; invariants; authority; risks. Anchor by default.
It owes youdesign.md, the file every later agent reads
4
/plan-feature
Phases, risks, tests and rollout from the design. No code. Ends in open questions.
You owe itanswers, written into design.md
For a game, step 3 is game-architect: it asks about gameplay, platform and the on-chain split, then writes concept.md and plan.md and waits for your approval.
For a hackathon, /add-skill startup-builder brings idea-sprint, positioning and pitch-deck; its idea.md is what /plan-feature starts from.
