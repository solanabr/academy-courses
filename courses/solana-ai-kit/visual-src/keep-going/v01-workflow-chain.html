A command per step, from backlog to merge

The agent workflow, and where it stops for you

/intake
PRs, issues, a goal, into a numbered work list and questions.
gate 1: if it has questions
/research
Parallel researchers plus an adversary. Dated, sourced facts in a file.
/decide
Settle a question by verified panel, or bring it to you as a brief.
/plan-work
Owned tasks with files, interfaces and a review gate each.
gate 2: always. Nothing is built before you approve
/implement
Runs the approved plan in worktrees from a pinned commit. No merges.
/review-round
Review by lens, High and Critical findings reproduced, numbered sheet.
gate 3: if anything is open
/fix-wave
The fixes you approved, applied and re-verified. At most two waves.
/ship
Merges one PR at a time, inside a standing order you gave, with checks green and a review on the head commit.
only you can type it
/crew runs these seven and pauses at the gates
/rundown for a returning user: what was decided while you were away, what needs your call, what is in flight, what is next.
/report and /decide write a local HTML page under .claude/reports/ with what to read or answer.
Start smaller. /plan-feature and /quick-commit first. Reach for /crew when a backlog is real and you have a day to watch it.
