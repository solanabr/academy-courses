Question at the gate
Built with a database and a web app, what breaks?
One question, three honest answers. Only the candidate where something breaks needs a chain.
Candidate1
the credit tab
Breaks: the owner holds the record and the customer must trust it. A shared record neither party edits is the thing a chain supplies.
Passes

the supplier payment log
Nothing breaks: only the owner reads it, nobody to distrust.
Pivot: does anyone else need to read this record?
Fails
