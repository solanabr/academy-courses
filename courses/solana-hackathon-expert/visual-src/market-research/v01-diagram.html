Evidence a judge can click

Three kinds of evidence a judge can click, and what each one is allowed to prove

public platform count
Example source
earn.superteam.fun home: 213,730+ users and 2,680+ sponsors
read 2026-09-06
Proves
demand exists for a product of that kind
Does not prove
demand for yours
on-chain read
Example source
a program page on any explorer: transaction count (all time and last thirty days), time of the last transaction; a comparable's TVL where published
Proves
a competitor's real usage and when it went quiet; how much value people trust to that kind of product
Does not prove
address count equals people
sponsor listing
Example source
a side track or bounty on Earn with the count of applicants where shown
Proves
builders believed the sponsor's problem was worth a week
Does not prove
end users want it
every number count, URL, date read
